## What the scene does
- About 57% of pedestrians (random selection) lose their polyester jacket and are left in a cotton t-shirt.
- Bumper covers (polypropylene), taxi roof signs, and the phone case disappear. Vinyl ads (PVC) disappear too. The LED screens stay.
