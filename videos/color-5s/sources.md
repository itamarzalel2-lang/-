## What the scene does
A post-processing effect on the final image: rod-weighted luminance (red ≈ 0), saturation (glare), a mild blur (low acuity), and a gray spot in the center of the frame (the rod-free foveola). In the replay the effects are added one at a time, in sync with the narration.
