# Premise: air-5s

**One line:** `What if all air disappeared for 5 seconds?`

**Twist:** you survive (~10 seconds of consciousness), but the spit on your tongue starts to **boil**. The sound design is the star: Times Square cuts to dead silence, and all you hear is your own heartbeat.

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–3.5 | Street level, sunny, pigeons, city noise. Fact: 10 tonnes of air on every m² |
| 3.5 | Dead silence. Black sky with stars at noon, a bare Sun, black shadows, pigeons fall |
| 4.7 | Fact: black sky (Rayleigh) |
| 6.6 | Fact: 10 s of consciousness, the spit boils |
| 8.5 | The air is back: ears pop, the noise floods back, horns. CTA |
