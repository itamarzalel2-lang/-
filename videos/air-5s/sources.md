## Physics shown without numbers
- **Silence:** sound is a pressure wave in air. With no air, Times Square goes completely silent. What you'd still hear is your own body (heartbeat, a ring in your ears) through bone conduction, and that's all the soundtrack plays during those 5 seconds.
- **Harsh shadows:** without sky light (the light scattered by air) only direct sunlight remains, so shadows go nearly black. There's no haze either, so you can see sharply to the horizon.
- **The Sun:** a bare white disk with no halo. The halo around the Sun is scattering in air too.
- **Pigeons** fall: wings have nothing to push against. When the air comes back they fly again.
- **Cars:** combustion needs oxygen. Engines stall and drivers brake.
- **When it comes back:** the air returns in place, the pressure jumps back, and your ears "pop". Then the noise floods back in.
