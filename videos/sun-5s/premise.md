# Premise: sun-5s

**One line:** `What if the Sun disappeared for 5 seconds?`

**Premise check:** a well-known general idea ("what if the Sun disappeared"), but the usual angle is "forever". The "5 seconds" version with the precise payoff (exactly 5 seconds of darkness, 8 min 20 s late) is a fresh angle for the format.

**Twist:** in the moment itself, **nothing happens**. The light takes 8 min 20 s, so the darkness comes late, lasts exactly 5 seconds, and the return is late too. Bonus: gravity is also "late", so Earth keeps orbiting an empty spot.

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–3.2 | Aerial dive into a sunny Times Square, looking south toward the Sun |
| 3.5 | "Sun gone for…" counts up, but the Sun is still shining. "The Sun is gone. You can't tell." |
| 6.2 | Fact: 8 min 20 s |
| 8.6–10 | "8 minutes 20 seconds later…" card with a running clock |
| 10.0 | Instant darkness. The billboards are what's left. Cars brake, people stop |
| 11 | Fact: gravity at the speed of light, Earth orbiting an empty spot |
| 15.0 | The light comes back (also late). CTA |
