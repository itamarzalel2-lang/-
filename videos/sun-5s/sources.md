## Physics shown without numbers
- **Nothing happens at the moment it vanishes:** the light already on its way keeps arriving for 8 min 20 s, then stops for exactly 5 seconds (the photons that were never emitted), then comes back.
- **The dark is instant:** light doesn't fade out. It simply stops arriving (the transition is one frame).
- **Your eyes:** pupils open within a few seconds (the exposure in the scene rises a little), but full dark adaptation takes 20–30 minutes, so you won't see the stars in 5 seconds. See `blackout-5s/sources.md`.
- **The city lights** (billboards, windows) are on all the time. In the dark they're what's left.
