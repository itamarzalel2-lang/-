## Physics shown without numbers
- "What's actually up there": with no light pollution the stars and the Milky Way are there. The scene shows them as a long-exposure image, then "what you'd see" in 5 seconds: black, because the eyes haven't adapted.
- Cars: "every light" includes headlights, so drivers brake in the dark.
